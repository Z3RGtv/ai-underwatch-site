Voices of the world.

Our English voice samples and local character speech use the LibriTTS high-quality multi-speaker model published by Michael Hansen / Rhasspy for Piper. Character voice labels are fictional interface names, not the original readers' names.

The LibriTTS corpus was prepared by Heiga Zen, Viet Dang, Rob Clark, Yu Zhang, Ron J. Weiss, Ye Jia, Zhifeng Chen and Yonghui Wu (2019), from LibriSpeech / LibriVox recordings. Dataset: CC BY 4.0. These samples are synthetic derivatives; no endorsement by the dataset contributors is implied.
